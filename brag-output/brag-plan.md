# /brag Plan: StayGuard AI — Executive Demo & Pitch Video

## Creative Angle & Structure

A high-production IT project commercial and technical demo video structured into three clear narrative acts:
1. **WHY THIS PROJECT** (The Problem): The hidden $14B hospitality cancellation crisis.
2. **WHAT I HAVE BUILT** (The Solution): The complete production MLOps architecture & LightGBM pipeline.
3. **LIVE DEMO & ACTION** (The Product in Use): Interactive UI walkthrough with real-time radial risk gauges and automated deposit playbooks.
